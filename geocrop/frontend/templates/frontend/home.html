Welcome to GeoCrop

Satellite-powered crop intelligence, mapped in real time.

🌱🌾🌽🌾🌱

GeoCrop is a satellite-based crop intelligence platform. It takes satellite imagery of agricultural land and returns crop-related insights — whether the land is cropland, what crop is growing, how much area it covers, and an estimated yield — all visualized on an interactive map. The platform is being built and validated on USA agricultural data first, with adaptation to Pakistan planned next.
