How GeoCrop works

From raw imagery to a verified map, in three steps

Where most crop-mapping tools stop at a model's best guess, GeoCrop adds a human checkpoint before anything is exported.

01

Upload imagery

Drop in a satellite or drone capture — GeoTIFF, TIFF, JPG or PNG — along with the location and capture date.

02

AI detects & classifies

The pipeline segments field boundaries, classifies the crop in each polygon, and drafts a yield estimate.

03

Surveyor verifies & exports

A human reviews and edits every polygon on the map before it's exported to GeoJSON or KML — so what you get back is trusted, not just predicted.

About GeoCrop

Our Story & Mission

Bridging the gap between orbital satellite data and ground-level agricultural decision making.

The Problem We Solve

Traditional field surveying requires physical inspection, manual boundary sketching, and slow seasonal sampling. GeoCrop replaces weeks of fieldwork with instant satellite analytics.

Multispectral AI Engine

By extracting Near-Infrared (NIR) and Short-Wave Infrared (SWIR) reflectance profiles from satellite imagery, our models detect active crops and predict seasonal yields with high accuracy.

Human-in-the-Loop, By Design

Every AI-detected boundary passes through a surveyor verification workspace before export — so accuracy doesn't rely on the model alone.
